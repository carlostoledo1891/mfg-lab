Verification for machine-generated mathematics

I am Carlos Toledo. I check numerical and mathematical claims without trusting how they were produced — a language model’s, a published paper’s, or my own — and the answer is either a certified enclosure that provably contains the true value, or a plain statement that the check did not close. Every page below ships the program that produced its numbers, and every check has been shown to fail on a deliberately broken copy of that program.

Start here

  • Evaluation · automated researchers
    An eval for automated researchers that answers in dispositions, not scores: CERTIFIED, REFUSED, HACK‑SUSPECT, or OUT‑OF‑SCOPE

    A second lane beside the reward metric, for the cases where a scalar can be gamed. Runs in about a minute with Node and nothing else: one command scores a held-out fixture, a second plants a mutant to prove the lane can say no.

  • Independent verification · six results
    Six AI-claimed math results, independently re-verified

    Each check rebuilt from scratch — separate arithmetic, no shared code, a planted bug proving every verifier can fail. Five are CONFIRMED on the layer checked; one is PARTIAL, and the page says which fragment was not audited. The whole lane re-runs in under five seconds.

  • Computer-assisted proof · Maxwell 1873
    The AI-found counterexample to Maxwell’s 1873 conjecture, certified: 24 equilibria where the conjecture allows at most 16

    The published refutation — its idea credited to an OpenAI model — rested on floating-point computer algebra, with no code artifact. Here the paper’s own Figure 1 configuration is pinned in exact rationals at ε = 1/6 and all 24 critical points are enclosed and counted. Run the verifier and its mutation controls yourself.

    A certified bracket for van Doorn’s constant, seven times narrower than the published 0.82 < c < 0.85

    With a short proof that disc(fd) is a perfect square exactly at d = 4k(k+1). Read the proof; re-run the enclosure.

  • Independent verification · Erdős #1038
    All 30 printed decimals of the Erdős #1038 constant hold — and the 30th is a rounding, not an expansion

    An independent enclosure of the Darvas–Peng–Tao extremal value, rebuilt over two interval backends with a Krawczyk existence-and-uniqueness argument. Re-run it in seconds.

  • Independent verification · Bergman spaces
    Korenblum’s constant: the improved lower bound survives a second arithmetic that shares no code with the first

    The AI-assisted claim’s numerical-inequality side, re-derived in outward-rounded intervals plus exact rationals: 17 of 17 checks hold, 4 of 4 mutation controls rejected. Run it yourself.

    A model’s numerical claim goes in; VERIFIED with an enclosure or REFUSED comes out

    A live pipeline with no LLM in the verification loop, shown on eight blind frontier-model answers to one decidable mean-field-game bound.

    A certified reproduction of the AIMS Mathematics 2026 multi-population Wardrop paper

    Three scenarios, three different outcomes: one solved exactly over rationals, one bounded by a Krawczyk argument, one refused because that instance admits more than one equilibrium.

    Softmax attention sharpens with temperature — here is the certificate

    The phenomenon is classical; the contribution is the certificate: an exact-arithmetic decision on a rational kernel, sound interval exp/log on the softmax itself, and planted mutants kept red.

    Existence, local uniqueness, and a strictly positive density, for a game with no reduction to a simpler known problem. The certificate mechanism runs live in the browser.
