# Carlos Toledo — computer-assisted proof and validated numerics

> Computer-assisted proofs and validated numerics for number theory, mean-field
> games, network equilibria and price formation. Where a paper reports a converged
> iterate, this site returns a **certificate** — an interval or exact-rational enclosure of
> the equilibrium, with its location and local uniqueness proved — or an explicit
> **refusal** where the instance is degenerate. Written by Carlos Toledo (Brazil),
> built on the open-source `eqcert` kernel (MIT).

## What the distinction means

A solver that stops moving tells you its residual is small. It does not tell you
that a solution exists, that it is the only one nearby, or that the density stayed
positive. A **tolerance** is a promise about a solver; a **certificate** is a
statement about the problem. This work produces the second, using outward-rounded
interval arithmetic and exact rational arithmetic, and refuses when the instance
will not support a certificate.

Every certificate ships with **falsifiers**: deliberately broken variants that must
fail. A certificate that cannot go red is treated as fake.

## Verifiable claims

These figures are reproducible by running the verifier that ships with the paper.
They are measurements, not marketing numbers.

- Congestion mean-field game (a = 1/2, sigma = 0.5, A = 0.3, N = 14): existence,
  local uniqueness **within the even (cosine) subspace**, and strictly positive
  density, certified in interval arithmetic. Enclosure radius r = 7.75e-15,
  certified density floor min m >= 0.9736, Z1 = 0.5268. Seven falsifiers, all red.
  The standalone Python verifier uses the standard library only, no dependencies,
  and runs in 2.31 s. An independent JavaScript implementation agrees bit-for-bit
  (difference 0.00e+00).
- Multi-population Wardrop equilibrium: an independent, proof-carrying reproduction
  of Bakaryan, Aoun, de Lima Ribeiro, Hovakimyan and Gomes, *Distributed
  Hessian–Riemannian flow for multi-population Wardrop equilibrium*, AIMS
  Mathematics 11(5) 15143–15162 (2026), doi:10.3934/math.2026623. One scenario
  refuses, one is exact over BigInt rationals, one is a Krawczyk enclosure.
- Price formation with a stock constraint (hydro water value on a scenario tree):
  certified by zero-gap LP duality. The sign of the duality gap is load-bearing —
  weak duality makes a negative value structurally impossible, not a tolerance.

## What is deliberately NOT claimed

- A result certified at an instance is not a theorem about a model.
- Local uniqueness certified within a subspace is stated as within that subspace.
- No claim of peer review. No ladder word ("proved", "certified") is used as a
  status for this work without a named human signature behind it.
- Search-based literature negatives are null results and are labelled as such.

## Who it serves

- **Research groups** — independent validation and stress-testing of a published
  result: reproduce it, then enclose it, and report honestly when a scenario refuses.
- **Industry and frontier systems** — certification of numerical and optimization
  claims that decide real money, including energy price formation under a stock
  constraint.
