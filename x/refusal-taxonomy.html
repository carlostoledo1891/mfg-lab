Frontier path 01 of 05 · The classification

The refusal taxonomy

This tree has been building a theory of when computation must refuse, using MFG as the test bed, and describing itself as an MFG certification lab. Six kinds of refusal are already sitting in it, unnamed as a class.

Status — read this before anything below

Nothing on this page is claimed, certified, enclosed or proved. No kernel, no certificate, no falsifier, no ledger record, and no literature gate has run on it. It names a direction and what would have to be true. A prospectus that reads like a result is the defect, so this one says so at the top and is not published.

Why this is the load-bearing one

The geometric-vs-representation split found on 2026-07-30 was recorded as “a distinction”. It is the first two entries of a classification. What makes it research rather than a survey is that each kind has a DIFFERENT remedy — so a decidable discriminator between them is an instrument, not a description. Everything else in this set of five becomes easier to state once this vocabulary exists.

The sixth arrived from outside MFG entirely, which is the first evidence that the classification is about computation rather than about this tree's subject matter.

The first decidable claim

Given a failed enclosure, the kind of refusal is decidable — there exists a test, computable from artefacts the solver already produces, that separates geometric from representation refusal without human judgement. Candidate: nullity of the linearisation under exact rational arithmetic (geometric) versus norm-invariance of the failure locus under a change of weight (representation, already measured flat across five norms).

what would falsify it

Two instances the test cannot separate, or one it separates wrongly — a case with positive nullity whose failure nonetheless retreats when the basis is enriched.

Scored, and the honest risk

N / A

N: LOW. Classification is not deep mathematics. A: VERY HIGH and unverified. The asset is that this tree has collected refusals for months, which nobody does, because everybody ships successes.

A taxonomy with no discriminator is a survey with a table. The whole thing stands or falls on at least one decidable test. If no kind can be told from another except by reading the transcript, this is prose and should be labelled prose.
