Sandbox draft 09 · Metastable clustering · latent bifurcation certification

A certified critical threshold for attention clustering

Feasibility verdict — THE ONE — feasible and aligned

The only one of the four that is both feasible here and aligned with what this tree already does — and it is the geometry thesis applied to attention. Attention dynamics on N tokens are a finite-dimensional dynamical system; certifying where a bifurcation occurs means certifying where an eigenvalue crosses zero, bracketed by interval arithmetic. That is exactly the machinery in eqcert, and exactly the shape of the existing refusal-frontier work (closes at A = 5, refuses at A = 6).

RESCORED 2026-07-30 — CONFIRMED — and the novelty-zero finding stops being an objection

The first verdict on this page was scored on ONE axis: is the mathematics new. That axis contradicts this programme's own record. mfg-congest is not new mathematics — it is the first validated ENCLOSURE of known mathematics. wardrop-repro is explicitly a REPRODUCTION. stock-constraint certifies an LP duality argument a century old. None of the delivered work here is novel mathematics and all of it is valuable, so killing new ideas by a standard the existing work would itself fail was an error.

Academic novelty: ZERO — Geshkovski et al. prove the clustering theorem; Lessard-Pugliese make certified bifurcation brackets routine.

Artifact value — does a CHECKABLE version exist? HIGH. No certified, re-runnable, finite-n bracket for an attention system exists as an artifact. The method is off-the-shelf; the artifact is not.

Under the corrected scoring this was never damaged. 'We applied standard validated-continuation machinery to a new object AND SHIPPED IT RE-RUNNABLE' is the whole product.

What it is

Attention layers form semantic clusters. In mean-field particle systems, clusters appear through phase transitions at critical interaction parameters — a smooth distribution splitting into metastable groups. The proposal is to certify where that happens.

The claim, and whether it is decidable

the claim

For a specific finite attention model: the critical parameter at which clustering onsets lies in a certified bracket [c₋, c₊] — a location, not an asymptotic statement. Decidable, small, and attackable.

is there a certificate here WE could build?

Yes, and it reuses the stack unchanged: interval arithmetic, an enclosed eigenvalue crossing, a bracketed threshold, and a falsifier that plants a model whose true threshold is outside the claimed bracket.

occupancy check, 2026-07-30 — confirmed feasible, and priced honestly

The asymptotic theorem is OCCUPIED DEFINITIVELY. Geshkovski, Letrouit, Polyanskiy and Rigollet prove tokens cluster as t → ∞, with the limiting object determined by the spectrum of the value matrix (arXiv:2305.05465, NeurIPS 2023; companion in Bull. AMS 2025). Their theorem may not be restated as a finding.

And the METHOD is occupied too — this is the pattern that killed the sheaf proposal. Lessard & Pugliese (arXiv:2404.00535, Linear Algebra Appl. 728 (2026)) give a complete template: two-parameter continuation, Newton–Kantorovich for rigorous existence, a Gershgorin argument that exactly one eigenvalue has zero real part, and a rigorous enclosure of the normal-form coefficient. So what is left is an INSTANCE, not a method, and the mathematical novelty is zero. The honest framing is "we applied standard validated-continuation machinery to a new object", never "we developed a certification technique".

Why it is still the one: with prescribed weights the self-attention flow is a finite-dimensional ODE with analytic right-hand side — no training is needed to define the object, because the theorems are about fixed, time-independent weights. Interval exp, exact rationals and Krawczyk are what eqcert already holds.

Three costed caveats: enclosure width grows with nd, so this lives at small n and d = 1,2, not model scale; the softmax makes the field rational-in-exponentials, needing care where the denominator is near-degenerate; and prescribed weights are NOT a trained Transformer — any sentence implying "we certified when model X collapses" is forbidden.

The narrowing goes into the claim BEFORE work starts: prescribed weights, finite n, certified parameter bracket. Strip any of the three and it collapses into their theorem plus their method.

The honest risk

Two real ones. First, the asymptotic result is likely already proved — Geshkovski, Letrouit, Polyanskiy and Rigollet have rigorous work on clustering in self-attention dynamics; if the critical threshold is in there, this dies. Second, and this is the failure mode that killed the sheaf proposal: validated continuation and rigorous bifurcation analysis are a mature generic technology (Zgliczyński school, CAPD). If pointing an existing validated continuation tool at attention dynamics is the whole contribution, that is an application note, not a discovery. Occupancy check commissioned; this verdict is provisional until it returns.
