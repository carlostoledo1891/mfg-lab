erdős #106 · exact arithmetic · verification of a posted witness

The posted k=16 configuration, machine-checked to the last digit.

Roles first, so nothing blurs: the configuration is Bojan Basić's; it was posted and attributed by StijnC on the erdősproblems.com discussion thread for problem #106 (post-8433, 12 Aug 2026); this page contributes only an exact-arithmetic check of that post's configuration, independent of the poster's own arithmetic — every decision in ℚ and ℚ(√2), no floats in any decision path, re-run live in your browser below. Not refereed.

⚠ Why this page is gated. It checks an unrefereed forum post, and the problem's own record is in motion: the site's proof-claims subpage has carried a full claimed disproof at k=4 since 29 Jul 2026 (Conner Silverstein; checked in exact fractions by its author, and independently re-checked with Sage by StijnC on 11 Aug 2026 — their work, not checked here), and a thread comment notes that claim would give f(k²+1)>k for every k≥4. The k=16 configuration below was offered by its own post as a simpler example for large k, and had no posted verification when this page was built. This page stays in the tree, unindexed, until the owner decides reach.

The problem, quoted erdősproblems.com/106 · accessed 2026-08-19

“Draw n squares inside the unit square with no common interior point. Let f(n) be the maximum possible sum of the side-lengths of the squares. Is f(k²+1)=k?” — problem source [Er94b] per the page; the axis-parallel variant g(k²+1)=k was proved by Baek, Koizumi and Ueoro (arXiv:2411.07274v2). The page notes, of its own comment section, both “There are no solutions, partial or complete, claimed in the comments” and “1 claimed proof for this problem”; the two statements are quoted here as found, and reconciling them is the maintainers' business, not this page's.

The check, live runs in this page · exact arithmetic · no network

The kernel below rebuilds the posted configuration from its exact coordinates (all vertices rational; only the rotated square's side length is irrational, 10√2⁄33), then decides five statements in exact arithmetic: the count is 257 = 16²+1; each shape is a genuine square; every square lies in the closed scaled box [0,16]²; all 32,896 pairs have disjoint interiors (separating-axis over exact edge normals; boundary contact allowed); and the side-length total exceeds 256 strictly, which reduces to 10√2 > 14, i.e. 200 > 196. Two deliberately broken variants must be rejected for the page to show green — a check that cannot go red is decoration.

If either variant were accepted, the board would show red on that row and the page's verdict would be void.

What the verified inequality does — and does not — say

Unscaled, the verified statement reads: 257 squares fit in the unit square, pairwise interior-disjoint, with side-length total (8434 + 10√2)⁄528 — that is, f(257) ≥ 16 + (10√2 − 14)⁄528, strictly above 16, because f is a maximum over all admissible configurations and a valid witness bounds it from below. Under the problem page's own definition, and under two stated readings (closed containment in the unit square; “no common interior point” read as pairwise disjoint interiors), that is the k=16 instance of the question answered in the negative by Basić's configuration. It says nothing about any other k: the monotonicity of k(f(k²+1)−k) noted on the problem page was not checked here, and the problem's status line belongs to the site's maintainers, not to this page.

Method · provenance · disclosure

Arithmetic: exact rationals over BigInt; ℚ(√2) elements as pairs a + b√2 with the sign of a + b√2 decided by sign analysis and squaring (√2 irrational, so a + b√2 = 0 only at a=b=0). Interior-disjointness by the separating-axis criterion for convex polygons over exact edge normals, boundary contact allowed. Floats appear only in display strings marked approx, never in a decision. The checker was authored with Claude (Fable 5) inside this shop's gated workflow and runs as deterministic exact arithmetic; the same kernel is re-run headless by the unit battery on every make check. Disclosure follows the thread's own norm. This page states its check plainly and copies the humility token of the problem's other claimants: Not refereed.

What this page does not claim

It does not claim the construction (Basić's), the posting or attribution (StijnC's), the k=4 claim or its consequences (Silverstein's and its checkers'), any statement for k ≠ 16, any change to the problem's status, or any refereed standing. The word “verification” on this page always means: the arithmetic statements about the one posted configuration, decided exactly, with controls that prove the checker can reject.
