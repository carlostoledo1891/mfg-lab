What it is

Optimal transport asks for the cheapest way to move one distribution onto another; its dual gives potentials whose gap certifies optimality. This tree already solves and certifies a stock-constrained instance of exactly that problem — the water-value report prices a finite resource across time and scenarios by zero-gap LP duality.

Why it is a frontier

This is not a direction, it is an audit of something already delivered. Measured 2026-07-30: 200/200 deterministic instances and 120/120 tree instances certify, worst duality gap 1.33e-14, complementary slackness and the dual wedge signs checked, and three planted mutants caught by the battery. Calling that "a field we might enter" would be false modesty in the wrong direction.

The first decidable claim

Not a research programme — one statement that is either true or false, small enough to be attacked. Everything else follows from having one of these.

discrete claim — DISCHARGED 2026-07-31

The float duality gap is now both an exact rational zero (S2) and an interval containing 0 with explicit upper bound r = mag(signedGap) under outward rounding (S4). The open claim that remains is continuum only.

what the discrete certificate asserts

Exact-rational residuals ≡ 0 including signedGap; interval wrap of the float LP-duality objectives so zero gap survives outward rounding. Both gated under check-sin.

what would falsify the discrete wrap

What must exist first

Discrete half done. The float kernel ships and is byte-identity gated; the exact-rational twin and the interval wrap now sit beside it under check-sin. What remains for a published report is the continuum statement (Laguerre / Monge–Ampère with discretization error) — a different size of work.

The honest risk

The risk here is the opposite of the other four: not that the ground is taken, but that the step is small enough to be unremarkable. Wrapping an existing zero-gap certificate in intervals is competent engineering, not a discovery. The genuinely open item is the CONTINUUM statement, which the report already names as open — off the finite tree, exactness stops being free and the analytic literature (Monge–Ampère regularity) is deep and populated.

Literature occupancy

An occupancy check is a precondition, not a formality. No claim here may be called ours until a literature gate has returned a verdict on this specific niche — not on the field, which in every one of these five is old and well populated, but on the narrow question of whether certified, rigorously enclosed results already exist in it. Any of these five may be killed by that check, and killing four of five now would be a good outcome.
