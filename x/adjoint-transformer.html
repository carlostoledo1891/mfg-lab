Sandbox draft 07 · Adjoint-matched continuous-depth · MFP = MHJBT

The exact discrete adjoint we already certify, pointed at depth

Occupancy check, 2026-07-30 — KILLED, and it overturned this page's first verdict

This page originally said "already built here" and treated that as an advantage. The occupancy check says the idea is dead, for a reason neither I nor the proposal saw.

1 — the MFG half is a numbered remark in the standard survey. Achdou & Laurière, arXiv:2003.04444 §2 Remark 6, read at body level: "The operator M ↦ −ν(ΔhM)i − 𝓕i(U,M) is the adjoint of the linearization of the operator U ↦ …" Not merely standard — the defining structural feature of the scheme, with its own numbered remark.

2 — and the fatal one: backpropagation already IS the exact discrete adjoint. For any differentiable forward map, reverse-mode automatic differentiation is the transpose of forward-mode by construction — the chain rule transposed. So Mbackward = MforwardT holds exactly, at every layer, for free. "Enforcing exact discrete duality layer by layer" enforces a property the training algorithm cannot violate. There is nothing to enforce.

3 — the gradient claim is false on both halves, as suspected here and now adjudicated. Adjointness gives ⟨Mu,m⟩ = ⟨u,Mᵀm⟩, which holds for every linear operator's transpose — content-free as a conservation statement; conserving ‖x‖² needs skew-adjointness. And since σ(Mᵀ) = σ(M), enforcing adjointness guarantees the backward blow-up matches the forward blow-up rather than removing it. Removing it needs σ ≈ 1, i.e. orthogonality — a different constraint, owned by another line of work.

What remains true: this tree really does certify FP = HJBᵀ at operator level with two mutants caught. That capability is real. It is just not a frontier, because the property it certifies is one nobody can break.

ATTRIBUTION UNRESOLVED, and it must not be asserted. The survey credits the scheme to [MR2679575], which did not resolve. My recollection said Achdou & Capuzzo-Dolcetta; the page's prose says Achdou & Laurière. Two records disagree and neither is established — this repo has already shipped one false attribution to the co-author it was about.

What it is

Treat a Transformer's forward pass at infinite depth as Fokker–Planck transport and backpropagation as the adjoint HJB, then enforce that the discrete operators are exact transposes layer by layer.

The claim, and whether it is decidable

the claim

The decidable one is small and we can already do it: given a discrete forward operator and its backward partner, MFP = MHJBT exactly over rationals — an exact matrix identity, decidable, no tolerance. What is NOT decidable without experiments is any claim about training behaviour.

is there a certificate here WE could build?

Yes, and it is small. Port the existing adjoint-matching certificate to a Transformer-shaped operator and certify the transpose identity exactly. Honest framing: that is a transfer, not a discovery, and its value is entirely in whether the ML side cares.

The honest risk

The adjoint structure of finite-difference MFG schemes is, to my understanding, standard — Achdou & Capuzzo-Dolcetta's monotone scheme has the FPK operator as the exact transpose of the linearised HJB by construction. If so, the mathematics is prior art on both sides and only the framing is new. Occupancy check owed before this is called ours.
