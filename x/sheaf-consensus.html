What it is

A cellular sheaf attaches a vector space to each node and edge of a graph plus restriction maps between them; its Laplacian measures how far local data are from agreeing globally. The kernel is exactly the globally consistent states.

Why it is a frontier

Multi-agent systems fail by disagreeing silently. A sheaf Laplacian's spectral gap is a rate: how fast local disagreement must decay. That is the quantity an orchestration engineer actually wants and rarely gets as a guarantee.

The first decidable claim

Not a research programme — one statement that is either true or false, small enough to be attacked. Everything else follows from having one of these.

the claim

For a given discrete sheaf on a finite graph: the smallest non-zero eigenvalue of the sheaf Laplacian is at least c > 0, with c explicit — hence a certified consensus rate.

what a certificate would assert

A rigorous interval enclosure of the spectral gap, and an exact-arithmetic determination of the kernel dimension, so "how many independent global sections exist" is answered exactly rather than numerically.

what would falsify it

An eigenvalue below c, exhibited by an eigenvector whose Rayleigh quotient is certified below the claimed floor.

What must exist first

Exact restriction maps over rationals, a coboundary operator that is verified against the definition rather than assumed, and an eigenvalue enclosure that does not silently assume symmetry.

The honest risk

Rigorous eigenvalue enclosure is a mature craft — the contribution would be the sheaf formulation and the consensus reading, not the enclosure technique. If that reading is already standard in the ACT literature, the honest outcome is to say so and stop.

Literature occupancy

An occupancy check is a precondition, not a formality. No claim here may be called ours until a literature gate has returned a verdict on this specific niche — not on the field, which in every one of these five is old and well populated, but on the narrow question of whether certified, rigorously enclosed results already exist in it. Any of these five may be killed by that check, and killing four of five now would be a good outcome.
