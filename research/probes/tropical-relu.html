Academic novelty: ZERO — certified robustness over min-max representations is published (arXiv:2310.04916), and min-max IS the tropical form.

Artifact value — does a CHECKABLE version exist? HIGH, and this is the miss. alpha,beta-CROWN and its peers emit a VERDICT from a large solver. The verdict is not independently checkable — you trust the tool. A zero-dependency certificate for a small network, re-runnable by a stranger in seconds, DOES NOT EXIST and is exactly what this tree builds.

The honest pitch is not 'a new verification method'. It is 'the incumbent's answer, made checkable by someone who does not trust the incumbent'. That is a smaller and far more defensible claim, and it is this programme's thesis applied to NN safety. Scope: small networks, where completeness is affordable.

What it is

The tropical semiring replaces addition with min and multiplication with +. Under it a polynomial becomes a piecewise-linear convex function — and a feedforward ReLU network is exactly a tropical rational function, not an approximation of one. The network's decision boundary is therefore a polyhedral object with an exact combinatorial description.

Why it is a frontier

It converts a black box into a geometry. Instead of sampling a network to look for adversarial examples, the active-facet structure can in principle be enumerated, which is the difference between "we did not find one" and "there is none".

The first decidable claim

Not a research programme — one statement that is either true or false, small enough to be attacked. Everything else follows from having one of these.

the claim

For a fixed small ReLU network N, a point x₀, and a radius ε: no input within the ε-ball of x₀ changes the network's argmax class.

what a certificate would assert

An interval enclosure of every active tropical facet within the ball, and the sign of the margin on each — so the conclusion holds for every real input in the ball, not for the ones that were sampled.

what would falsify it

A single input inside the ε-ball that flips the class. The falsifier is a search that must be run against a deliberately mis-certified network and shown to find it.

What must exist first

An exact min-plus layer representation over rationals; an interval facet enumerator; and a margin bound that survives outward rounding. None of these exist here yet.

The honest risk

This is the most crowded of the five and the honest risk is that it is already solved. Certified adversarial robustness is an established field with strong incumbents — αβ-CROWN, DeepPoly, Marabou, MILP encodings — several of them competition-winning. A tropical formulation earns its place only if it certifies something they cannot, or certifies the same thing with a structurally simpler falsifier. Until that comparison is run and lost or won, this page claims nothing.

Literature occupancy

An occupancy check is a precondition, not a formality. No claim here may be called ours until a literature gate has returned a verdict on this specific niche — not on the field, which in every one of these five is old and well populated, but on the narrow question of whether certified, rigorously enclosed results already exist in it. Any of these five may be killed by that check, and killing four of five now would be a good outcome.
