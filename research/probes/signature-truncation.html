What it is

Rough path theory (Lyons) gives a calculus for signals too irregular to differentiate — Brownian motion, tick data, jittery sensor streams. The signature of a path is its sequence of iterated integrals: a compact encoding of the path's geometric shape, invariant to time-warping.

Why it is a frontier

Signatures are the feature map underneath a generation of continuous-time sequence models. In practice the expansion is truncated at some level N, and the truncation error is almost always quoted asymptotically — an order, not a number you could put in a risk report.

The first decidable claim

Not a research programme — one statement that is either true or false, small enough to be attacked. Everything else follows from having one of these.

the claim

For a path X given by its samples and a truncation level N: the truncated signature differs from the true signature by at most r in a stated tensor norm, with r an explicit number.

what a certificate would assert

An interval enclosure of the truncation remainder, plus exact discrete satisfaction of Chen's identity S(X)s,t = S(X)s,u ⊗ S(X)u,t over rationals, so composition does not accumulate drift.

what would falsify it

A path and a level N at which the claimed bound is violated, found by search; and a Chen residual that is non-zero in exact arithmetic.

What must exist first

A rational tensor-algebra kernel, an enclosure of the factorial decay with its constants made explicit rather than absorbed, and a decision about which norm the claim is stated in.

The honest risk

The asymptotic theory is classical and complete; the open part is producing a number for a given path rather than a rate. Whether that is genuinely unclaimed is exactly what the occupancy check has to answer — and if numerical rough-path libraries already ship rigorous remainder bounds, this dies.

Literature occupancy

An occupancy check is a precondition, not a formality. No claim here may be called ours until a literature gate has returned a verdict on this specific niche — not on the field, which in every one of these five is old and well populated, but on the narrow question of whether certified, rigorously enclosed results already exist in it. Any of these five may be killed by that check, and killing four of five now would be a good outcome.
