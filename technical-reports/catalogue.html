Every direction, with its verdict

One place for the whole research surface — including the dead ones. A catalogue that lists only survivors is a sales sheet; the kill reasons are the reusable part, and two of them have already prevented a second mistake. Named here 2026-08-20 — that sentence stood without its witnesses, which is the defect this page exists to avoid: one is the rotor kill (an interval rotor is a SET, and a set cannot satisfy R R† = 1 exactly unless it is a point) — which names a constraint that recurs whenever someone proposes exactness inside an enclosure, and is recorded here as a standing constraint rather than as a second kill, because this page can point at no later direction it has actually stopped; the other is the infeasibility kill (an idea whose proof is an experiment cannot be certified in this tree), which killed mf-jepa and then supplied the second of the two independent kills on w2-latent-loss. Naming them corrected the count in the sentence above, which is kept as written: one of the two has actually prevented a second mistake; the other states a constraint that would, and has not yet been called on to. Both rows are below, with their reasons unedited. Verdicts here are this shop’s own judgements, not certificates — the certified work is on the reports page, which since 13 August 2026 carries finished work only and points here for everything still being worked out.

How to read it

Verdicts are this repo's own judgement, and the occupancy half is separate from the feasibility half. A direction can be perfectly good mathematics and still be infeasible here — this tree does validated numerics and ships zero-dependency verifiers; it cannot train models. Two entries below died on that alone. Three turned out to be things already delivered in this programme under another name, which is the most common outcome so far and the most useful one.
